How we evaluate

Practical criteria, stated plainly.

Recommendations are built around intended use and the trade-offs a prospective user should understand—not universal rankings.

  1. 01FitThe job a tool helps with and the audience it may serve.
  2. 02WorkflowGetting started, core tasks, and working alongside other tools.
  3. 03ClarityRelevant capabilities and limitations in straightforward language.
  4. 04Ongoing reviewInformation revisited when a product materially changes.
